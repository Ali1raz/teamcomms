# Article template

## Frontmatter

```mdx
---
title: {What the reader will do, for example "Invite someone to an organization"}
description: {One sentence that says what the article helps the reader do}
---
```

Use the labels the app actually shows. A docs title can be more descriptive than a button, but never invent a menu name, button, or path.

## Skeleton

```mdx
---
title: {Action-oriented title}
description: {One sentence}
---

{What the reader will do and when it is useful. Two short paragraphs at most.}

## Before you begin

{Required role, membership, or setup. Omit this section when there is nothing to check.}

## Steps

### 1. {Action-oriented step title}

{What to open or select, and what the reader should see.}

### 2. {Action-oriented step title}

{The next action and its result.}

### 3. {Action-oriented step title}

{The final action and its result.}

## What happens next

{The result, and any access or membership change the reader should expect.}

## Troubleshooting

{Only verified problems and fixes. Omit this section when there are none.}

## Related guides

- [Related article](/docs/path/to/article)
```

## Step pattern

Each step answers three things:

1. Where the reader is.
2. What they select or enter.
3. What they should see after they do it.

Example:

```mdx
### 2. Choose the team

Open the **Teams** list and select the team you want to invite someone to. The team's conversation opens.
```

Use bold only for interface labels, such as **Invite** or **Members**. Do not bold whole sentences.

## Rules

- One goal per article. Link to another guide instead of covering a second goal.
- Put steps in the order the reader performs them.
- Use a numbered list for a short procedure, or headings when steps need explanation.
- Put options, requirements, and outcomes in bullet lists.
- State the role or permission before a restricted action.
- Warn before an action that cannot be undone.
- Keep simple articles short. Leave out an optional section when it adds nothing.
- Do not present a planned feature as available.
