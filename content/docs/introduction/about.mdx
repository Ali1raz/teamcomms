### 1. Problem Statement

Businesses struggle to manage communication across multiple organizations and teams, leading to scattered conversations, poor organization, and difficulty controlling access to information.

### 2. Proposed Solution Overview

Teamcomms is a B2B communication platform that enables users to join multiple organizations and teams, exchange messages in team-specific conversations. It provides invitation-based membership and access control to keep conversations accessible only to authorized users.

### 3. Objectives of the Proposed System

- Enable users to manage multiple organizations through one account.

- Organize communication into teams and threads.

- Support organization and team invitations.

- Provide organization-wide and team-specific messaging.

- Enforce membership-based access control.

- Preserve message history when users leave organizations or teams.

- Improve collaboration and communication within businesses.
