---
title: Modules
description: Overview of the modules and features in TeamComms
---

Teamcomms can be divided into these main modules and features.

### 1. User & Authentication Module

- User registration and login

- Profile management

- Session management

### 2. Organization Management Module

- Create and manage organizations

- Join multiple organizations

- Organization member management

- Owner/admin/member roles

- Organization invitations

### 3. Team Management Module

- Create and manage teams

- Join multiple teams within organizations (when invited or added by an owner/admin)

- Add or remove team members

- Team-specific access control

- Team invitations

### 4. Messaging Module

- Send and receive messages

- Edit and delete messages

- Message timestamps and sender information

### 5. Invitation Module

- Invite users to organizations

- Optionally assign a team during invitation

- Accept or reject invitations

- Invitation expiration and revocation

### 6. Access Control Module

- Role-based permissions

- Organization and team membership validation

- Restrict access after leaving an organization or team

- Preserve historical messages for authorized members
