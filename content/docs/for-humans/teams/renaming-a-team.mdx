---
title: Rename a team
description: Change the name of a team in an organization
---

You can change a team's name at any time. The new name appears everywhere the team is listed, including the sidebar and the Teams page.

## Before you begin

- You must be an owner or admin of the organization.

## Steps

1. Find the team in the sidebar, or on the organization's **Teams** page.
2. Open the team's three-dot menu and select **Edit**. On the Teams page, open the menu on the team card and select **Edit**.
3. In the Update Team dialog, change the **Name** field. The name must be between 2 and 20 characters.
4. Select **Update Team**.

Teamcomms shows a confirmation message and the team uses the new name.
