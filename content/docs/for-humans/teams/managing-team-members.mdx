---
title: Manage team members
description: View the members of a team, and add or remove team members
---

Team members are the people who can open a team and read and send its messages. Members of the organization who are not in the team cannot see the team.

## Before you begin

- You must be an owner or admin of the organization to add or remove team members.
- Team members can view the member list but cannot change it.

## View the members of a team

1. Open the team from the sidebar or the organization's **Teams** page.
2. Select **Total members** on the team card, or select **Manage Members** in the team's three-dot menu.

The Members page lists each team member with their name, role in the organization, email, and whether they are online.

## Add members to a team

1. On the Members page, select **Add Member**. You can also open the team's three-dot menu and select **Add Member**.
2. In the Add Member to Team dialog, select the checkboxes for the organization members you want to add. Use **Select All** to choose everyone who is not already in the team.
3. Select **Add**.

Members who are already in the team show as checked and cannot be selected again. Teamcomms shows "Members added successfully!".

## Remove a member from a team

1. On the Members page, open the three-dot menu on the member you want to remove.
2. Select **Remove**.
3. In the Confirm dialog, select **Remove Member**, or select **Cancel** to leave it.

Teamcomms shows "[Member name] removed from team".

## What happens next

- Added members can open the team and its conversations right away.
- A removed member loses access to that team's conversations.
- Removing someone from a team does not remove them from the organization. They stay an organization member and keep their other teams.
