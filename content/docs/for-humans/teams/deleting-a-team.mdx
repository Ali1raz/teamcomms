---
title: Delete a team
description: Delete a team and its conversations from an organization
---

Deleting a team removes it for everyone. The team's conversations and messages are deleted too, and this cannot be undone.

## Before you begin

- You must be an owner or admin of the organization.
- Make sure the team's members no longer need its messages. Deleting a team cannot be undone.

## Steps

1. Find the team in the sidebar, or on the organization's **Teams** page.
2. Open the team's three-dot menu and select **Delete team**. On the Teams page, open the menu on the team card and select **Delete team**.
3. In the Delete Team dialog, select **Delete Team**, or select **Cancel** to leave it.

Teamcomms shows "[Team name] Deleted successfully!".

## What happens next

- The team and its messages are gone for everyone.
- The team disappears from the sidebar and the Teams page.
- If you were viewing that team, Teamcomms moves you back to the organization.
- The people who were in the team stay organization members. Only the team is removed.
