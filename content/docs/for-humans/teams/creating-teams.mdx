title: Create a team
description: Create a team in an organization and choose its name
---

A team is a group within an organization where members talk in one place. Create a team to separate work, such as one team per project or department.

A team belongs to one organization, and its conversations are visible only to that team's members.

## Before you begin

- You must be an owner or admin of the organization. Members cannot create teams.
- Open the organization first: open your user menu (your avatar), select **Dashboard**, then open the organization.

## Steps

### 1. Open the Create Team dialog

In the sidebar, select **Create Team**. On the organization's **Teams** page, you can also select **Create Team** when the list is empty. The Create Team dialog opens.

### 2. Name the team

In the **Name** field, type a name between 2 and 20 characters. As you type, Teamcomms shows the name that will be used in links, in the line "will be created as".

### 3. Create the team

Select **Create Team**. Teamcomms creates the team, adds you as a member, and opens the team.

## What happens next

- The team appears in the sidebar and on the Teams page for its members.
- Only team members can see the team and open its conversations. Other organization members do not see it.
- You are a member, so you can send messages in the team right away.
- Owners and admins can add other organization members to the team, or rename or delete it.
