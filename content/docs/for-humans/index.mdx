## Use Cases of TeamComms:

### Freelancers working with multiple clients

**Use case**: A freelance web developer works with three different businesses simultaneously.

**How it works:**

- Creates or joins a separate organization for each client.
- Joins relevant teams, such as Development or Design.
- Communicates with each client through team conversations.
- Keeps messages and discussions separated by organization.
  **Example:** A developer working for a clothing store and a restaurant can discuss website bugs with the clothing store without exposing those conversations to the restaurant.

### Business consultants working with multiple companies

**Use case**: A business consultant advises several companies on strategy, operations, or growth.

**How it works:**

- Participates in each company's organization.
- Joins teams relevant to the consulting engagement.
- Shares recommendations, discusses problems, and coordinates tasks with authorized members.
- Maintains separate communication histories for each company.

**Example:** An HR consultant advising two companies can discuss recruitment with Company A's HR team and organizational restructuring with Company B's management team.

Start with your organizations. An organization is a workspace for your company, business, or group. You can belong to more than one organization.

## What you can do

- Create an organization and become its owner.
- Keep more than one organization and switch between them.
- See how many members and teams each organization has.
- Invite people to an organization by email if you are an owner or admin.
- See all invitations and cancel a pending one if you are an owner or admin.
- Accept or reject an invitation to join an organization.
- Create teams and manage their members if you are an owner or admin.
- Send messages in a team, and edit or delete your own messages.
- React to messages with emoji.
- Update your display name and profile image.
- See where you are signed in and sign out other devices.
- Update an organization's name and logo if you are an owner or admin.
- Leave an organization, or delete one if you are its owner.
