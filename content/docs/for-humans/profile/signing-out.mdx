---
title: Sign out of Teamcomms
description: Sign out of Teamcomms on this device
---

Signing out ends your session on this device only. Your profile and your organizations are not affected.

## Before you begin

- You must be signed in.

## Steps

1. Select your avatar in the top-right corner to open the account menu.
2. Select **Logout**.

On the public Teamcomms site, you can instead select **Logout** in the navigation bar.

Teamcomms shows "Successfully signed out!" and signs you out on this device. To use Teamcomms again, sign in with GitHub or Google.

## What happens next

- This device must sign in again to reach your organizations and teams.
- Your other devices stay signed in.

## Troubleshooting

- To sign out of your other devices, open your profile, go to the **Sessions** tab, and sign out or revoke them there.
- "Failed to sign out": check your internet connection and select **Logout** again.
