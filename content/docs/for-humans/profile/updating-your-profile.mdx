---
title: Update your profile
description: Change your display name and profile image
---

Your profile holds your display name and profile image. Other people see them next to your messages and in your teams.

Your profile page opens on the **Profile** tab by default, where you can also see your email address and the date you joined.

## Before you begin

- You must be signed in.
- You can only change your own profile.

## Steps

### 1. Open your profile

1. Select your avatar in the top-right corner to open the account menu.
2. Select **Profile**.

### 2. Change your name

In the **Update Your Profile** section, under **Name**, enter your new display name. Your name must be between 3 and 20 characters.

### 3. Change your profile image

1. Under **Profile Image**, select **Upload Image**.
2. In the dialog, choose an image or drag and drop it, then wait for it to upload.
3. To remove your current image and go back to the default avatar, select **Remove**.

Your image must be a supported image file no larger than 1 MB.

### 4. Save your changes

Select **Update Profile**. Teamcomms shows "Profile Updated successfully".

## What happens next

- Your new name and image appear next to your messages and anywhere your profile is shown.
- Changes take effect as soon as you save.

## Troubleshooting

- "Name must be 3 chars long." or "Name must be less than 20 chars.": shorten or lengthen your name to fit the limits and select **Update Profile** again.
- "Error uploading file!": the file is larger than 1 MB or is not an image. Choose a smaller image file.

## Good to know

- Your email address is shown on your profile but cannot be changed here.
- Teamcomms signs you in with GitHub or Google, so there is no password to manage on your profile.
