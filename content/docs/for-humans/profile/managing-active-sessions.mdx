---
title: Manage your active sessions
description: See where you are signed in and sign out other devices
---

A session is a place where you are signed in, such as a browser on your laptop or your phone. Your profile lists your active sessions so you can sign out of any device you do not recognize.

## Before you begin

- You must be signed in.
- Open your profile: select your avatar in the top-right corner, select **Profile**, then open the **Sessions** tab.

## See your active sessions

Under **Active Sessions**, each session shows:

- The browser and operating system, for example "Chrome on Windows".
- The IP address of the device.
- When the session was last active and when it expires.

Your current session is highlighted. Its button reads **Sign out**. Every other session shows **Revoke**.

## Sign out another device

1. Find the session you want to end.
2. Select **Revoke**.

Teamcomms shows "Successfully revoked session." That device is signed out right away and must sign in again.

## Sign out this device

1. Find your highlighted current session.
2. Select **Sign out**.

Teamcomms signs you out of Teamcomms on this device, and you must sign in again to use it.

## Sign out all other devices

1. In the **Danger Zone**, select **Sign out from all other devices**.
2. In the dialog, select **Sign out all**.

Teamcomms shows "Successfully revoked all other sessions." Every other device must sign in again. Your current session stays signed in.

## Good to know

- Revoking a session does not delete your account or change your profile. It only signs that device out.
- After you revoke a session, that device signs in again with GitHub or Google.
