---
title: Leave an organization
description: Leave a TeamComms organization and lose access to it and its teams
---

Leaving an organization removes you from it. You lose access to the organization and to all of its teams and messages. This cannot be undone.

Your other organizations are not affected.

## Before you begin

- The only owner of an organization cannot leave it. If you are the only owner, add another owner or delete the organization instead.
- Check that you no longer need anything in the organization.

## Steps

1. Open the organization you want to leave: open your user menu (your avatar), select **Dashboard**, then open the organization.
2. On the Teams page, select **Leave Organization**.
3. In the Confirm dialog, select **Leave Organization**, or select **Cancel** to stay.

Teamcomms shows "Organization left successfully!" and opens the Organizations page.

## What happens next

- You are removed from the organization and from its teams.
- You can no longer open the organization or any of its teams and messages.
- Your membership in your other organizations does not change.
- An owner or admin can invite you again later.

## Troubleshooting

- If you are the only owner of the organization, you cannot leave. Teamcomms shows "Failed to leave organization". Add another owner or delete the organization instead.
