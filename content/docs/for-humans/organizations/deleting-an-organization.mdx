---
title: Delete an organization
description: Delete a TeamComms organization and all of its teams and data
---

Deleting an organization removes it for everyone. All of its teams, conversations, messages, and members are deleted too. This cannot be undone.

## Before you begin

- Only the owner of the organization can delete it.
- Make sure you no longer need the organization and its data. Deleting an organization cannot be undone.

## Steps

1. Open your user menu (your avatar) and select **Dashboard**. The Organizations page opens.
2. On the organization's card, open the three-dot menu.
3. Select **Delete Organization**.
4. In the Confirm dialog, select **Delete Organization**, or select **Cancel** to leave it.

Teamcomms shows "Deleted organization successfully!" and removes the organization from the list.

## What happens next

- The organization and all of its data are deleted. This includes every team, every conversation and message, and all membership and invitation records.
- Every member loses access to the organization and its teams.
- The deletion cannot be reversed.

## Troubleshooting

- If you are not the owner of the organization, you cannot delete it. Ask the owner to delete it.
