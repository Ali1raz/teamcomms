---
title: Manage organization invitations
description: See every invitation an organization has sent and cancel a pending one
---

The Invitations page lists every invitation an organization has sent. Use it to keep track of who was invited, see each invitation's status, and cancel a pending invitation.

## Before you begin

- You must be an owner or admin of the organization. Members cannot manage invitations.

## Steps

### 1. Open the Invitations page

Open the organization, then select the small arrow next to **Invite** and choose **See All invitations**. The Invitations page opens.

### 2. Review the invitations

The table lists every invitation for the organization. Each row shows:

- **Invited User**: the person's name and email address. The name stays empty until that person has a TeamComms account.
- **Role**: the role the person will get when they accept.
- **Status**: **pending**, **accepted**, **rejected**, or **canceled**.
- **Invited By**: the owner or admin who sent the invitation.
- **Team**: the team included in the invitation, or empty if none.
- **Created At** and **Expires At**: when the invitation was sent and when it expires.

Select **Invite new Member** on this page to send a new invitation. Select **Refresh** to reload the list.

### 3. Cancel a pending invitation

1. Open the three-dot menu on the invitation row.
2. Select **Cancel invitation**.
3. In the Confirm dialog, select **Yes, cancel invitation**, or select **No, keep it** to leave it.

Teamcomms shows "Invitation canceled successfully!".

## What happens next

- The invitation's status changes to **canceled** and it stays in the list.
- A canceled invitation cannot be accepted. The person loses the link in their email.
- You can invite the same person again later with a new invitation.

You can cancel an invitation only while its status is **pending**. The **Cancel invitation** option is unavailable for accepted, rejected, and canceled invitations.
