---
title: Edit or delete a message
description: Change or remove a message you sent in a team
---

You can change or remove any message you sent. You cannot edit or delete messages that other people sent.

Changes appear for everyone in the team right away, without a refresh.

## Before you begin

- You can only edit or delete your own messages.
- Open the team that contains the message.

## Edit a message

1. Open the three-dot menu on your message and select **Edit**.
2. Change the text, and add or remove an attachment if you want.
3. Select **Update**, or select **Cancel** to keep the original.

Teamcomms shows "Message updated successfully" and all team members see the new version.

## Delete a message

1. Open the three-dot menu on your message and select **Delete**.
2. In the Confirm dialog, select **Delete**, or select **Cancel** to keep the message.

Deleting a message cannot be undone. Teamcomms shows "Message deleted" and the message disappears for everyone in the team.

## What happens next

- Edited and deleted messages update for every team member in real time.
- Deleting a message also removes its replies, reactions, and image.

## Troubleshooting

- If you do not see **Edit** or **Delete**, the message was sent by someone else. You can only change your own messages.
