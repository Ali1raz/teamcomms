---
title: React to a message
description: Add or remove an emoji reaction on a message in a team
---

A reaction is an emoji you add to a message, such as 👍 or ❤️. Reactions show how team members responded without sending a written reply.

Reactions update in real time. When anyone reacts, the change appears for everyone in the team right away.

## Before you begin

- You must be a member of the team that contains the message.

## Steps

### 1. Find the message

Open the team and find the message you want to react to. Each message shows its reactions below the text.

### 2. Add a reaction

1. Select the add reaction button (a smile with a plus) at the end of the reaction row.
2. Choose an emoji from the list.

Teamcomms shows "Reaction added" and the reaction appears on the message for everyone.

### 3. Remove or change your reaction

- To remove your reaction, select the reaction you added. Teamcomms shows "Reaction removed".
- To change it, choose a different emoji.

You can add one reaction per message. Choosing the same emoji again removes it, and choosing a different emoji replaces it.

## How reactions look

- Each reaction shows the emoji and the number of people who chose it.
- Reactions you added are highlighted. Hover over one to see whether it will add or remove your reaction.
