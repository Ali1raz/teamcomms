---
title: Send a message
description: Send a message in a team, with optional formatting and an image
---

Messages are posts in a team conversation. Everyone in the team can read and reply to them.

Messages update in real time. When you or anyone else sends, edits, or deletes a message, it appears for everyone in the team without refreshing the page.

## Before you begin

- You must be a member of the team. Open the team from the sidebar or the organization's **Teams** page.

## Steps

### 1. Open the team

Open the team you want to post in. The conversation opens, and the message box is at the bottom of the page.

### 2. Write your message

Select the message box and type your message. You can format the text with the toolbar above the box.

### 3. Optionally attach an image

Select **Attach**, choose an image, and it appears as a small chip above the send options. To remove it before sending, select the chip's delete control. You can attach one image to a message.

### 4. Send it

Select **Send**. The message appears in the conversation right away and is delivered to the team.

## Messages update in real time

- New messages from other team members appear automatically, with no need to refresh.
- When you are scrolled up and new messages arrive, a **New messages** button appears. Select it to jump to the latest message.
- Online indicators next to a person's avatar show who is currently online.

## What happens next

- The message is now in the team conversation for all team members.
- Team members can react to it and reply to it.

## Troubleshooting

- If your message contains sensitive information such as a credit card number or phone number, Teamcomms blocks it and shows a warning. Remove that information and select **Send** again.
